Information handling

Privacy.

The project brief does not send enquiries or store personal details. Information is used only on the page to generate a brief and is lost when you reload or leave it.

Your geographic selection is retained temporarily in this browser tab to transfer it from the map to the brief. Use “Clear selection” to remove it. Names, email addresses and other personal details are never placed in that storage.

The map uses geographic data included in the website. It does not request your location or load third-party maps. The hosting platform may process technical data required to provide access and operate the website.

Before direct enquiry delivery is enabled, the controller, purposes, legal basis, providers, retention periods and applicable rights procedures will be added.
