Practical information

Frequently asked questions.

What does the end-to-end service include?+

The stages agreed in writing: search, viewings, checks, legal and tax coordination, purchase management, renovation when required and handover. Responsibilities and third-party costs are specified.

How is a property requiring works managed?+

The renovation is built into the overall project plan: technical assessment, budget, permits, procurement, monitoring and handover. If you prefer to purchase first and carry out the works later, the timetable is adapted without losing continuity of the file.

How are fees calculated?+

Before work begins, you receive a proposal with fees, their calculation basis, payment milestones and third-party costs. This preview does not yet publish final commercial terms.

Can I manage the process from another country?+

We can coordinate the search and administrative tasks remotely. Steps requiring your presence, representation or specific documents are established with the professionals involved.

Do you only search advertised properties?+

No. Alongside portals and agencies, we use owners and local collaborators. Unadvertised opportunities depend on each search.

Is the initial consultation free?+

Yes. The first online consultation is complimentary and without obligation. It allows us to understand your objectives and consider the scope of the appointment.

Does the preview form send enquiries?+

Not yet. It prepares a sample brief that you can copy, including the selected area. It does not book a meeting or email your information.
