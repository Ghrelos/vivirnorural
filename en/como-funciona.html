Our services.

A coordinated service to find, buy and, when needed, renovate your home in Galicia.

An end-to-end service with one point of contact. We organise every stage, coordinate specialists and track budgets and timelines. You make informed decisions; we take care of putting them into practice.

Renovation and handover
01

Initial consultation and project brief

We review your objectives, preferred location, overall budget and timeframe. The first online consultation is complimentary and establishes the services your project requires.

  • Define requirements and priorities.
  • Agree which stages you want to delegate.
  • Written proposal covering scope, fees and third-party costs.
Request a consultation
02

Property search and selection

We identify and shortlist homes through portals, agencies, owners and our local network.

  • An active search based on the agreed brief.
  • Viewings and documentation of shortlisted options.
  • Assessment of the surroundings, access, services and property requirements.
03

Due diligence and specialist advice

We organise document checks and the specialists required for the transaction before you make purchase commitments.

  • Coordinate land registry, cadastral and planning checks.
  • Arrange technical assessment of the property and potential works.
  • Coordinate property, legal and tax advice relevant to your circumstances.
04

Negotiation and purchase completion

We manage communication and follow the transaction through, subject to your decisions and approval at each stage.

  • Support with price and terms negotiations.
  • Coordinate contracts, documents and the notarial signing.
  • Follow up taxes, registration and post-completion tasks within the agreed scope.
05

Renovation and handover

When the home needs renovating, we manage the entire process: design, budgets, permits, appointments and execution through to handover. Every significant decision is submitted for your approval.

  • Coordinate technical design, quotations, permits and paperwork.
  • Select and coordinate contractors and specialists.
  • Monitor progress, costs, timelines and issues, with regular updates.
  • Review handover, documentation and outstanding works with the responsible professionals.

Detailed oversight, even from a distance.

Follow the works without travelling: progress reports, photographs, updated budgets, schedules and an issues record. Changes are documented and submitted for your approval before being carried out.

Our service includes coordinating appropriately qualified specialists where needed. It does not replace their regulated roles or those of notaries, registries or public authorities. Each appointment and cost is specified before engagement.
