Why work with us?

A service for buyers who want to pursue a project in Galicia without having to manage every detail themselves.

The home and its surroundings are considered together.

01

The buyer comes first.

Your requirements define the search, not a fixed catalogue. Every option must demonstrate its relevance to your project.

02

Local knowledge and contacts.

Alongside conventional channels, we work with owners and local contacts to identify opportunities that have not yet been advertised.

03

One point of contact.

The same person leads your project from the first meeting to handover. You receive documents, viewing reports and project updates without needing to be on site.

04

Information for informed decisions.

We explain both the potential and the limitations of each option. The aim is a considered decision, not a purchase at any price.

05

Continuity after completion.

Our work does not end at completion. We manage any renovation the home needs and prepare it for use, with oversight through to handover.
